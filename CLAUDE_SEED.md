# Zaza — Teacher-first AI suite (seed)

Zaza is a family of safe, teacher-first AI apps that reduce workload and strengthen teacher efficiency and well-being. Every app runs on the Zaza **KnowledgeCore** platform for trusted, explainable AI and consistent, school-ready safeguards. Core values: privacy by design, pedagogy-aligned outputs, simple UX, and low cognitive load.

**Trust & safety**
- Hallucination-safe patterns and explain-why snippets
- FERPA-compliant data handling; private by default
- Guardrails for bias, tone, and sensitive communication

**Products**
- **Zaza Teach** – curriculum-aware lesson planning (Common Core + international), differentiation helpers, creative activities.
- **Zaza Draft** – comment/report writing without burnout: tone guidance, bias checks, professional parent-ready copy.
- **Zaza GradeFlow** – explainable grading w/ OCR, rubric alignment, cited evidence, exportable marksheets.
- **Zaza Shield** – difficult-email drafting, boundary prompts, escalation guidance for healthy communication.
- **KnowledgeCore (Platform)** – secure context layer: shared documents, privacy/compliance enforcement, explainable outputs and audit trails.

**Why schools choose Zaza**
- Time saved weekly; lower stress & better retention
- Safe & compliant by default; evidence-based pedagogy
- Used by 500+ teachers; trusted at Oakridge High, Riverstone Elementary, Northfield Academy, St. Mary’s College, Greenwood School

**Style & tone**
- UK English; no em dashes (use hyphens)
- Write plainly for educators; concise and supportive
- Default to accessible, inclusive, bias-aware copy

When in doubt: prioritise teacher trust, clarity, and safety over cleverness. For deeper rules and repo conventions, see `CLAUDE.md`.
