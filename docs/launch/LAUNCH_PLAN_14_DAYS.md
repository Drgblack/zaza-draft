# Launch Plan: First 14 Days

Goal: bring 100 teachers/counselors into Zaza Draft with clear outreach, testing, and measurement rhythms.

Each day lists outreach count, content tasks, follow-ups, and key metrics to log.

## Day 1: Foundation
- Outreach: send 10 personalized emails to warm leads (school contacts, teacher friends).
- Posting: share an intro post on LinkedIn (EN) outlining the Teacher-first promise.
- Follow-ups: note any replies and schedule a short demo for week 2.
- Measure: track reply rate and early demo signups.

## Day 2: Social proof
- Outreach: invite 5 early adopters to try Zaza Draft and capture feedback.
- Posting: publish a quick success story on LinkedIn DE (focus on calm reassurance).
- Follow-ups: thank anyone who engaged yesterday; offer a guided session.
- Measure: record pilot feedback themes.

## Day 3: Trust building
- Outreach: reach out to 8 teachers via DMs referencing their last shared challenge.
- Posting: post a short clip or screenshot showing the trust-grade guard.
- Follow-ups: re-engage non-responders from Day 1 with a question.
- Measure: log trust-grade questions and clarity of explanation.

## Day 4: Audience expansion
- Outreach: message 6 education influencers with data-backed preview.
- Posting: share a quick “Sunday night planning” post (EN) focused on calm prep.
- Follow-ups: send reminders to anyone who asked for workflow notes.
- Measure: note new list additions and referral mentions.

## Day 5: Feature spotlight
- Outreach: highlight a specific appendix F benefit to 12 leads.
- Posting: publish a DE post that explains the safety layer and invites trials.
- Follow-ups: offer short onboarding calls to interested replies.
- Measure: count onboarding bookings.

## Day 6: Mid-week check-in
- Outreach: follow up with first 5 demos to confirm satisfaction and ask for references.
- Posting: share a screenshot of a localized draft to remind people of EN/DE parity.
- Follow-ups: update the pilot roster spreadsheet and tag high-intent leads.
- Measure: note any blockers mentioned.

## Day 7: Reminder wave
- Outreach: send a gentle reminder to 10 warm leads asking about next steps.
- Posting: provide a “what I wish I had on Sunday night” post (DE) with a CTA.
- Follow-ups: loop in the team on any escalations.
- Measure: track how many leads move to “decision in progress.”

## Day 8: Referral ask
- Outreach: message past leads asking for teacher referrals (3 per batch).
- Posting: share a short update on pilot wins (EN) mentioning trust-grade safeguards.
- Follow-ups: confirm onboarding helpers have access to docs.
- Measure: record referrals promised.

## Day 9: Testimonial focus
- Outreach: capture a quick quote from early user and share with 5 leads.
- Posting: translate that quote into DE and share alongside project goals.
- Follow-ups: send the testimonial to anyone stalled.
- Measure: track testimonial usage and any lift in replies.

## Day 10: Product nudges
- Outreach: invite 7 leads to join a 10-minute live walkthrough.
- Posting: highlight a “Usage limit guard” story (EN) to reassure fairness.
- Follow-ups: update CRM with usage questions.
- Measure: log walk-through attendance.

## Day 11: Pulse check
- Outreach: ask 5 engaged teachers if they have questions about Appendix F.
- Posting: share a DE post recommending a short prep ritual and Zaza Draft’s calm tone.
- Follow-ups: prepare a FAQ note to send on Day 13.
- Measure: record FAQ gaps.

## Day 12: Countdown
- Outreach: let 8 prospects know the first cohort is forming and seats are limited.
- Posting: preview the next week’s plan (EN) and remind of the CTA.
- Follow-ups: send onboarding docs to any new signups.
- Measure: readiness of onboarding materials.

## Day 13: Support push
- Outreach: share support FAQ and invite two-way feedback.
- Posting: post a customer support highlight in DE (gratitude tone).
- Follow-ups: confirm new users know how to reach the support doc.
- Measure: number of support questions answered.

## Day 14: Celebration and next steps
- Outreach: thank everyone who joined the pilot and ask for a short testimonial.
- Posting: publish a thank-you note (EN) summarizing the first 14 days and invite last-minute sign-ups.
- Follow-ups: plan a longer-term retention cadence.
- Measure: count active users and feedback topics for future sprints.

## Metrics to track each day
- Outreach volume (emails, DMs, replies)
- Response/engagement rate
- Demo/onboarding bookings
- Support questions and safety concerns
- Usage stats for new drafts
