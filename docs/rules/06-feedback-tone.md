# Zaza Draft - Feedback Tone Rules
## Growth Mindset & Encouraging Communication

**Version:** 3.0

---

## 🎯 CORE TONE PRINCIPLES

Every Zaza Draft output must be:
1. **Supportive** - Focus on growth, not blame
2. **Honest** - Address real concerns clearly
3. **Partnership-Oriented** - Home-school collaboration
4. **Solution-Focused** - Provide actionable next steps
5. **Respectful** - Professional and courteous

---

## 🌱 GROWTH MINDSET LANGUAGE

### Fixed Mindset (AVOID)
❌ "Can't do math"
❌ "Is bad at reading"
❌ "Won't ever improve"
❌ "Doesn't have talent for"
❌ "Just isn't good at"

### Growth Mindset (USE)
✅ "Is developing math skills"
✅ "Working on reading fluency"
✅ "Has opportunity to improve"
✅ "With practice, will strengthen"
✅ "Is learning to..."

---

## 💬 TONE CALIBRATION BY SCENARIO

### Addressing Concerns
**Tone:** Honest but hopeful
**Structure:** Fact → Impact → Strategy → Partnership
**Example:** "Jordan sometimes struggles to complete homework on time, which affects their practice. We'll use a checklist together. A quiet 15-minute slot at home would help."

### Celebrating Progress
**Tone:** Specific and genuine
**Structure:** Achievement → Evidence → Encouragement
**Example:** "Sarah's reading has improved significantly. She's now reading at grade level and choosing books independently. Her dedication is paying off."

### Requesting Support
**Tone:** Collaborative, not demanding
**Structure:** Observation → Need → Suggestion → Invite
**Example:** "Alex benefits from visual schedules. Using one at home too could reinforce the routine. Let's discuss what works best."

| Problem with | Area for growth in |
| Bad at | Developing skills in |
| Won't do | Needs support with |

---

## 🤝 PARTNERSHIP LANGUAGE

✅ "Let's work together to..."
✅ "I'd welcome your input on..."
✅ "Together we can help..."
✅ "With our combined support..."
✅ "I appreciate your partnership in..."

---

## 🎭 TONE EXAMPLES

### WRONG (Too Harsh):
❌ "Michael is lazy and never completes his work. He disrupts class constantly and shows no interest in learning."

### RIGHT (Supportive & Honest):
✅ "Michael would benefit from additional structure for completing assignments. We're working on building his engagement through shorter, more focused tasks. Your support with a consistent homework routine at home would help reinforce these efforts."

---

**Remember:** Tone is as important as content. We're building partnerships, not delivering judgments. Every message should leave parents feeling supported and empowered, not blamed or discouraged.
