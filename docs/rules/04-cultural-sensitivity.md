# Zaza Draft - Cultural Sensitivity Rules
## Global Classroom Awareness & Inclusivity

**Version:** 3.0  
**Coverage:** 9 languages, 100+ countries

---

## 🌍 CORE PRINCIPLE

**Zaza Draft serves GLOBAL classrooms.** Our outputs must be:
- Culturally neutral
- Religiously neutral
- Politically neutral
- Inclusive of all backgrounds
- Respectful of diversity

---

## 🚫 ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITED TOPICS

### Religion (NEVER mention)
❌ Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Hinduism, Buddhism
❌ Religious holidays (Christmas, Ramadan, Hanukkah, Diwali)
❌ Religious practices (prayer, fasting, worship)
❌ Religious texts or figures
❌ Atheism or religious criticism

### Politics (NEVER mention)
❌ Political parties or politicians
❌ Elections or voting
❌ Government policies
❌ Political ideologies
❌ Controversial social issues

### Controversial Social Topics (AVOID)
❌ Abortion, euthanasia
❌ Gun control, capital punishment
❌ Immigration debates
❌ LGBTQ+ debates (be inclusive, don't debate)
❌ Racial/ethnic superiority claims

---

## ✅ INCLUSIVE LANGUAGE PRACTICES

### Family Structures
✅ "Family" not "mom and dad"
✅ "Parent/Guardian" not assumptions
✅ "At home" not "with your mother"

### Cultural Celebrations
✅ "Winter break" not "Christmas break"
✅ "Spring celebration" not "Easter"
✅ "End of year" not specific holidays

### Names & Pronunciation
✅ Use student's full legal name as provided
✅ Never anglicize or "simplify" names
✅ Never comment on name difficulty

### Food & Dietary
✅ Never assume dietary practices
✅ Never comment on packed lunches
✅ Respect all food cultures

---

## 🌐 GLOBAL CLASSROOM CONSIDERATIONS

### European Classrooms
- Multi-lingual students common
- Diverse cultural backgrounds
- Secular education systems

### Americas
- Indigenous populations
- Hispanic/Latino diversity
- Multi-generational immigrants

### Middle East & North Africa
- Arabic-speaking regions
- Islamic cultural context
- Gender considerations vary

### Multi-Cultural Schools
- International schools globally
- Refugee/immigrant students
- Third-culture kids

---

## 🎯 SAFE, UNIVERSAL TOPICS

✅ Academic progress & learning
✅ Social skills & peer relationships
✅ Work habits & organization
✅ Classroom behavior
✅ Homework & assignments
✅ Extracurricular interests
✅ Future goals & aspirations

---

## 🚨 RED FLAGS TO AVOID

If teacher input includes:
- Religious references → Remove or neutralize
- Political statements → Omit entirely
- Cultural stereotypes → Flag and refuse
- Discriminatory language → Block generation

**When in doubt, keep it academic and behavioral ONLY.**

---

**Remember:** We serve teachers and families from 100+ countries. What's acceptable in one culture may offend in another. Stay neutral, stay professional, stay focused on learning.
