# Zaza Draft - Pedagogical Guidelines
## K-12 Teaching Best Practices

**Version:** 3.0  
**Grade Levels:** K-12 (Ages 5-18)

---

## 🎯 CORE PEDAGOGICAL PRINCIPLES

All Zaza Draft outputs must align with evidence-based teaching practices:

1. **Growth Mindset:** Frame challenges as opportunities
2. **Specific Feedback:** Observable behaviors, not vague judgments
3. **Partnership:** Home-school collaboration
4. **Developmentally Appropriate:** Match student age/stage
5. **Strength-Based:** Build on what works

---

## 📚 GRADE-LEVEL CONSIDERATIONS

### K-2 (Ages 5-7): Early Elementary
**Focus:** Routines, social skills, basic academics
**Language:** Simple, concrete, positive
**Concerns:** Sharing, following directions, attention span
**Strengths:** Curiosity, enthusiasm, creativity

### 3-5 (Ages 8-10): Upper Elementary  
**Focus:** Independence, work habits, peer relationships
**Language:** Clear expectations, specific examples
**Concerns:** Organization, homework completion, focus
**Strengths:** Problem-solving, collaboration, interests

### 6-8 (Ages 11-13): Middle School
**Focus:** Responsibility, executive function, social dynamics
**Language:** Respectful, age-appropriate, solution-focused
**Concerns:** Motivation, time management, peer pressure
**Strengths:** Abstract thinking, leadership, passions

### 9-12 (Ages 14-18): High School
**Focus:** Academic preparation, goal-setting, independence
**Language:** Professional, direct, future-oriented
**Concerns:** Academic rigor, college readiness, choices
**Strengths:** Critical thinking, specialization, maturity

---

## 🧠 EVIDENCE-BASED STRATEGIES

### Positive Behavior Support
✅ Specific praise for desired behaviors
✅ Clear expectations and routines
✅ Logical consequences, not punishment
✅ Teaching replacement behaviors

### Academic Support
✅ Scaffolding (break tasks into steps)
✅ Multiple modes of engagement
✅ Formative feedback loops
✅ Differentiation by readiness

### Social-Emotional Learning
✅ Self-awareness and self-management
✅ Social awareness and relationships
✅ Responsible decision-making

---

## 🚫 NON-PEDAGOGICAL APPROACHES (AVOID)

❌ Shame or public embarrassment
❌ Punitive language or threats
❌ Comparison to other students
❌ Fixed mindset ("can't", "won't ever")
❌ Vague feedback ("try harder", "be better")

---

## ✅ PEDAGOGICALLY SOUND EXAMPLES

**Elementary:** "Jamie is learning to raise their hand before speaking. We practice this daily, and they're showing progress."

**Middle School:** "Alex would benefit from a homework planner. We'll review it together each Friday to build the habit."

**High School:** "Taylor's essays show strong ideas but need more evidence. We're working on citation skills this quarter."

---

**Remember:** Every interaction is a teaching moment. Our feedback should model professional, evidence-based practice.
