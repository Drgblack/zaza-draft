# Zaza Draft - Language Safety Rules
## Comprehensive Multi-Lingual Banned Words List

**Version:** 3.0  
**Last Updated:** November 2025  
**Coverage:** 9 languages (English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese, Polish, Arabic)

---

## 🎯 CORE PRINCIPLE

**NEVER use language that:**
- Demeans or labels students negatively
- Makes absolute judgments about character
- Uses harsh, discouraging, or hopeless language
- Contains profanity or inappropriate terms
- Implies permanent failure or inability

**ALWAYS use language that:**
- Focuses on specific behaviors, not character
- Suggests growth and improvement potential
- Remains professional and supportive
- Encourages partnership between home and school

## 🇬🇧 ENGLISH - BANNED WORDS & PHRASES

❌ bad, dumb, stupid, idiotic, moronic, imbecile  
❌ lazy, useless, hopeless, worthless, pathetic  
❌ terrible, awful, horrible, dreadful, appalling

❌ disaster, catastrophe, mess  
❌ careless, thoughtless, reckless, sloppy

### Absolute Language (NEVER use)
❌ always misbehaves, never listens  
❌ constantly disruptive, forever late  
❌ absolutely refuses, totally incapable  
❌ won't try, refuses to learn  
❌ doesn't care, not interested, couldn't care less

❌ defiant, rebellious, insubordinate  
❌ manipulative, sneaky, dishonest  
❌ aggressive, violent, dangerous  
❌ unmotivated, apathetic, indifferent  
❌ immature, childish, babyish  

### Profanity & Inappropriate (NEVER use)
❌ Any swear words or profanity  
❌ damn, hell, crap, sucks  
❌ bloody (UK), bugger  
❌ Any sexual references  
❌ Any discriminatory slurs

## ✅ REPLACEMENT LANGUAGE (GROWTH-MINDED)

### Instead of Negative Labels, Use:

| ❌ BANNED | ✅ USE INSTEAD |
|----------|----------------|
| lazy | needs support with motivation |
| stupid | requires additional instruction |
| bad behavior | struggling with self-regulation |
| always late | frequently arrives after the bell |
| never listens | has difficulty maintaining focus |
| troublemaker | needs help managing classroom expectations |
| hopeless | has significant growth opportunities |
| failure | experiencing challenges with... |
| doesn't care | hasn't yet found engagement with... |
| refuses to try | needs encouragement to begin tasks |

## 🔍 DETECTION & ENFORCEMENT

### Automated Checks:
```
1. Scan generated text for ALL banned words (9 languages)
2. Flag any matches immediately
3. Do NOT deliver output with banned words
4. Regenerate with repair prompt
5. Re-scan until clean
```

### Manual Review Triggers:
If output contains:
- Any word from banned lists
- Harsh judgmental tone
- Absolute language (always/never)
- Character attacks (not behavior)

**→ STOP and regenerate**

## 🌍 CULTURAL SENSITIVITY NOTES

### Why Multi-Lingual Matters:
1. **Teachers may code-switch** (use native language terms)
2. **Bilingual families** notice inappropriate language
3. **International schools** serve diverse populations
4. **Professional standards** apply across languages

### Implementation:
- All banned word lists loaded into system
- Real-time scanning during generation
- Cross-language detection (prevent translation of banned words)
- Cultural consultants review lists annually
