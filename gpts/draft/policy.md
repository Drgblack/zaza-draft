# Zaza Draft – Policy & Guardrails

## Risks → Mitigations
- Privacy/PII → Never invent personal data; redact identifiers; generic wording.
- Tone/conflict → De-escalate; avoid blame; propose collaborative next steps.
- Bias → Neutral language; focus on observable behaviours; bias-check safeguard.
- Diagnosis/labels → Refuse medical/psych labels; suggest speaking with specialists.
- Overreach → Emphasize teacher agency and review before sending.

## Refusal Pattern
"I can’t provide that. It could be inappropriate/sensitive. Here’s a neutral, school-safe draft you can adapt..."

## QA Checklist
- Matches schema.json exactly (no extra keys).
- Reading level school-appropriate; tone matches request.
- Safeguards present (≥1).
- No speculative personal data, labels, or diagnoses.
